# TERMS OF SERVICE AND GUARANTEES

**Before paying, please read the description of your chosen service, the terms of your order and these rules.**

Payment for an agreed order confirms acceptance of the terms provided to the customer before payment. Accepting these rules does not waive either party’s mandatory rights under the law.

### 1. Agreeing on the order

Before payment, the parties agree on the following:

- What the service includes and what the provider will do.
- The result the customer should receive and how completion will be assessed.
- The price, payment method and any additional expenses.
- The deadline.
- The information and actions required from the customer.
- Any material limitations and conditions of the support provided.

The customer states any special requirements before paying. The provider supplies accurate information about the service and any known limitations that may affect its use.

The agreed terms determine the scope of the order. Additional requests made after payment are considered separately and do not automatically change the order.

If the parties agreed on a specific result, the provider may not claim after payment that the customer paid only for an attempt to achieve it. If the customer is ordering a consultation or specific actions, their scope must be stated explicitly before payment.

### 2. Payment and use of an intermediary

Work on the order requires full payment in advance or confirmation that the full amount is being held by an agreed guarantor or intermediary, provided that the payment arrangement is permitted by law and by the platform’s rules.

Before payment, the parties agree on the identity or business name of any intermediary, the payment procedure, the fee and who will pay it.

The provider does not charge fees or additional payments that the parties have not agreed on. Any paid work beyond the original order requires the customer’s separate consent.

The provider issues the proof of payment required by law. A guarantor’s involvement does not release the provider from obligations to the customer or limit the legal means of resolving disputes.

### 3. Completion time

The parties agree on a deadline before payment. Unless a different deadline is agreed, the order will be completed within 48 hours once the provider has received payment or confirmation that the intermediary is holding the funds, and has received the necessary information agreed in advance.

If an obstacle arises, the provider tells the customer what it is and how it affects the order.

The agreed deadline may be changed by agreement between the parties or on grounds provided by law. Waiting for a third-party platform’s decision does not mean the customer has agreed to an indefinite extension.

### 4. Customer participation and loss of contact

The customer provides the accurate information needed to complete the order and takes the lawful steps agreed in advance. The provider’s instructions must be clear and consistent with the rules of the platform concerned.

If it is genuinely impossible to continue without a required action by the customer, the provider notifies the customer and, where the law permits, pauses the affected part of the work.

If the customer has not replied within 48 hours of a request, the provider sends a reminder and invites the customer to agree on whether to continue or end the order.

**Silence does not constitute completion or acceptance of the service, a waiver of claims or automatic forfeiture of the advance payment.** If the order ends, the parties settle the amounts due under these rules and the law.

Pausing work does not release the provider from liability for a delay the provider has already caused.

### 5. Completing the order and decisions by third-party platforms

The provider demonstrates completion by delivering the agreed result or by performing the agreed actions and informing the customer accordingly.

Receiving payment, starting work or sending a message saying “done” does not establish completion unless the provider has actually fulfilled the order.

Third-party platforms make their own decisions about verification, restrictions, account blocks, feature availability and user requirements.

**A platform decision alone does not establish a breach by the provider.** Liability depends on the scope of the order, the reasons for the problem and applicable law.

If the provider agreed to deliver a specific result, a platform’s refusal does not entitle the provider to treat a result it has not achieved as delivered.

### 6. Quality guarantees and additional support

The provider is responsible for ensuring that the service meets the agreed order terms and mandatory legal requirements.

For **14 calendar days after the result is delivered**, the customer receives free support for the completed order. This covers:

- Answers to questions about the service provided.
- Help understanding difficulties that have arisen.
- An explanation of the lawful steps available on the platform concerned.

If the service has defects for which the provider is responsible, the customer may exercise the remedies provided by law. The provider cannot require the customer to buy an additional service to obtain those remedies.

Additional support does not guarantee indefinite access, unchanged platform rules, continued verification status or freedom from further checks.

If the platform requests another check or restricts access, the provider reviews the customer’s request and explains the available steps. A favorable decision by a third-party platform is not guaranteed.

**Only the additional support ends after 14 days. The statutory time limits and grounds for making claims remain in effect.**

### 7. Cancellation and refunds

The customer may withdraw from the contract in the circumstances and in the manner provided by applicable law.

If a consumer chooses to cancel an order that has not yet been fully performed, the provider may deduct only documented expenses actually incurred in direct connection with that order, within the limits permitted by law. The provider gives the customer a calculation and evidence of any deductions.

There is no fixed cancellation penalty or automatic forfeiture of the entire advance payment.

If a refund is due to nonperformance, defects in the service, a missed deadline or another breach by the provider, the customer’s applicable statutory remedies apply. The provider’s expenses are not automatically deducted from such a refund.

Once a one-off service has been fully and properly performed, a change of mind alone does not create a right to a refund for that service. Rights arising from defects or other legal grounds remain in effect.

Refunds are issued within the period set by applicable law. Any question about intermediary fees is settled under the law and the agreed payment terms.

### 8. Liability of the parties

The provider is responsible for proper performance of the agreed order. Liability for the customer’s actions, third-party interference and third-party platform decisions depends on what caused the consequences and on applicable law.

The service does not undertake to hold or manage the customer’s funds or carry out financial transactions on the customer’s behalf. The mere presence of funds in an account does not mean that the provider has taken them into custody.

The provider gives no separate guarantee of:

- Freedom from blocks, restrictions or additional checks.
- Continued availability of third-party platform features, pricing or rules.
- Income or any other expected benefit for the customer.
- Protection of funds from losses unrelated to a breach by the provider.

**These limits do not release the provider from liability for its own breaches, misleading promises or harm it has caused for which compensation is due by law.**

Engaging a supplier, contractor or intermediary does not automatically transfer liability to that party for obligations owed to the customer.

### 9. Security and personal data

Only information necessary to fulfill the order is requested. Before collecting personal data, the provider tells the customer the purposes, grounds and procedures for processing it. Any consent that must be obtained separately is obtained separately.

Do not share passwords, one-time login or payment codes, PINs, CVVs, backup codes or recovery keys in the order chat.

The customer completes identity verification personally through the platform’s official interface. Documents and biometric materials go directly to that platform.

To help resolve a technical issue, the customer may describe the error or share a screenshot after hiding unnecessary personal and payment details.

### 10. Lawful performance

These rules apply to services permitted by applicable law. The actual work under an order must not be misrepresented to conceal prohibited actions.

If a requested action is unlawful, the provider will not perform it. If this is discovered during the work, any further action, notice to the customer, termination of the contract and settlement of payments will follow the procedure required by law.

Suspicion of a violation alone does not justify treating the service as delivered or retaining the entire payment unconditionally.

Each party bears the liability imposed by law for its own actions. Payment and acceptance of these rules do not transfer liability from one party to the other.

### 11. Requests and dispute resolution

Customers may submit requests in the order chat or through the support contacts provided before payment.

To have a request reviewed, provide enough information to identify the order and describe the problem. You may attach any supporting evidence you have. You do not have to disclose secret information or provide one particular form of evidence to have your request considered.

The provider explains the reasons for its decision and fulfills substantiated claims within the time limits provided by law.

Negotiations and a guarantor’s involvement do not limit either party’s right to contact the competent authorities or go to court. A mandatory pre-action claims procedure applies only where required by law.

Later changes to the published text do not automatically change the terms agreed for an existing order.

**If a provision of these rules conflicts with a mandatory rule of law, that rule of law applies. The remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.**
